Universe Glitches Catalog

  • 1. Phantom Transit – A star shows transit dips with no visible planet.
  • 2. Echo Burst – Radio burst repeats with perfect timing, then stops.
  • 3. Frozen Pulsar – Pulsar briefly pauses its regular beat, then resumes.
  • 4. Color Swap Nebula – Nebula spectrum shifts bands overnight.
  • 5. Vanishing Dwarf – A faint star fades below detection, never returns.
  • 6. Mirror Signal – Narrowband signal mirrors Earth transmissions with delay.
  • 7. Tilted Orbit – Planet’s orbit precesses faster than models allow.
  • 8. Infrared Halo – Star surrounded by unexplained warm dust ring.
  • 9. Time-Lag Flare – Light flare appears before its predicted cause.
  • 10. Silent Supernova – Bright supernova with almost no neutrino output.
  • 11. Binary Drift – Binary stars slowly change separation without mass loss.
  • 12. Ghost Galaxy – Galaxy shows rotation but almost no visible matter.
  • 13. Broken Spectrum – Star’s spectrum has missing lines in key bands.
  • 14. Looping Meteor – Object appears to re-enter atmosphere multiple times.
  • 15. Static Void – Region of space with unusually low background noise.
  • 16. Hyperflare Ping – Single intense flare followed by faint repeating pings.
  • 17. Reversed Doppler – Source appears to move toward us and away simultaneously.
  • 18. Shadow Transit – Transit dip with no corresponding mass in follow-up.
  • 19. Spiral Jet – Black hole jet curves into a spiral instead of straight beam.
  • 20. Flicker Cluster – Star cluster brightness flickers in sync.
  • 21. Thermal Step – Planet’s temperature map shows sharp artificial boundary.
  • 22. Silent FRB – Fast radio burst with no afterglow or host galaxy.
  • 23. Phase-Locked Cloud – Gas cloud orbits in perfect resonance with a planet.
  • 24. Missing Eclipse – Predicted eclipse fails to occur once, then returns.
  • 25. Polar Ring Echo – Ringed galaxy emits repeating radio echoes from its ring.
  • 26. Drift Beacon – Narrowband signal slowly migrates across the sky.
  • 27. Inverted Transit – Star brightens slightly during a “transit” event.
  • 28. Glitching Quasar – Quasar brightness jumps in discrete steps.
  • 29. Silent Orbit – Object orbits star but reflects almost no light.
  • 30. Phase Shift Field – Region where multiple sources show synchronized phase shifts.
