What Are “Universe Glitches”?

“Universe glitches” refer to astrophysical events or observations that appear unusual, inconsistent, or difficult to explain using current scientific models. These anomalies often spark curiosity because they challenge our assumptions about how the cosmos behaves.

Examples of cosmic glitches

  • Stars that dim or brighten in patterns that defy natural explanations.
  • Radio bursts with strange timing or frequency structures.
  • Objects with trajectories that seem engineered or non-natural.
  • Infrared excesses that hint at hidden heat sources.

While most anomalies eventually receive natural explanations, some remain mysterious, motivating deeper investigation and new theories.

Why scientists study anomalies

Glitches help refine astrophysical models and reveal gaps in our understanding. Occasionally, they point toward new physics—or, in rare speculative cases, potential technosignatures from advanced civilizations.

This page catalogs conceptual examples of cosmic anomalies. It does not claim any confirmed extraterrestrial origin, but highlights why certain observations attract scientific interest.
